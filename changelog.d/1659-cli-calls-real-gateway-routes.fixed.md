`mcp-hangar status`, `add` and `remove` call routes the gateway serves. They read `/health`, posted to `/reload` and to `/MCP servers/{name}/stop`, none of which exists, so `status` always showed COLD from the config and `add` and `remove` never reloaded or stopped anything. They now use `/api/mcp_servers/`, `/api/config/reload` and `/api/mcp_servers/{id}/stop`, find the gateway from `MCP_HANGAR_URL` or `MCP_HTTP_HOST` and `MCP_HTTP_PORT`, send `MCP_HANGAR_API_KEY` as `X-API-Key`, and say why when the gateway refuses a call instead of carrying on.
