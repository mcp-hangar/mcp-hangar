The JSON-lines, CEF, LEEF and syslog audit exporters now write the caller and cost fields a tool call has. JSON lines: `caller_type`, `caller_id`, `cost_cents`, `cost_model`, `cost_input_tokens`, `cost_output_tokens`. LEEF and syslog: the same names in camelCase (`callerType`, `costCents`, ...). CEF: `suid` (caller id), `flexString2` (caller type), `cn2` (cost in hundredths of a cent), `cn3` (input tokens) and `flexNumber1` (output tokens), each labelled; the pricing model has no free CEF slot and is not written. A zero cost is written; a field the call lacks is left out. `error_type` is documented as written unchanged in every format.
