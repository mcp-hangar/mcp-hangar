**core:** A handshake-era (2025-06-18 and older) `POST /mcp` that carries an `Mcp-Method` or `Mcp-Name` header is answered in full again. The SEP-2243 front-door wrap replayed its body with an invented disconnect, so the SSE answer stopped before writing anything: the client got `200` and an empty stream, and uvicorn logged "ASGI callable returned without completing response". A repeated `Mcp-Method` or `Mcp-Name` is now refused with `400` and `-32020`, the same answer the 2026-07-28 entry gives.
