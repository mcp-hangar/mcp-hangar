**core:** A front-door flat `tools/call` now returns its upstream's result whole. With batch truncation on, it was cut like a `hangar_call` batch member, and the caller got an "upstream answered with a result this gateway cannot return" tool error instead of its result, with no continuation tool on that surface to fetch the rest. Over the 10 MB per-call size cap, the caller got an empty success with no word of what it lost. The flat call now takes its result whole, as the Python facade's `invoke` already did: batch truncation leaves it out of the budget, stores no continuation for it, and the per-call size cap does not drop it. `hangar_call` results are cut exactly as before.
