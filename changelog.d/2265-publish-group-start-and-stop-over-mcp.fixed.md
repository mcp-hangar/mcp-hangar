**core:** `hangar_start` on a group now publishes what the start recorded: each started member's `McpServerStarted` and the group's own events. The group started its members itself, outside the command bus, so those events stayed recorded until a later health sweep, call or start command drained them: in egress the members' tools were missing from the projection until then, a digest pin on them could not be checked, and under a `header_exposure` block that withholds, `hangar_call` refused them as `schema_unknown`. `hangar_stop` on a group and the delete of a group now publish the members' stops and the group's events the same way, instead of leaving them for a late log row.
