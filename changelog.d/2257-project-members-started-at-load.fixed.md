**core:** A group member that its group starts while the configuration loads, which `auto_start` (the default) does at boot and on a reload, now has its tools projected. Before, that start was recorded on the member and never published, so in egress the member's tools were missing from the projection until a later health sweep or call published it: a digest pin on it could not be checked, and under a `header_exposure` block that withholds, `hangar_call` refused every one of its tools as `schema_unknown`. The start is now published once the event handlers, withdrawals and pins are in place at boot, and once a reload has committed.
