**core:** A member that `POST /api/groups/{group_id}/members` adds to a group with `auto_start` (the default) now has its tools projected when the request returns. The group started the member, but the handler published only the group's events, so the member's start stayed recorded on it until a later health sweep, call or start command published it: in egress its tools were missing from the projection until then, a digest pin on them could not be checked, and under a `header_exposure` block that withholds, `hangar_call` refused every one of them as `schema_unknown`.
