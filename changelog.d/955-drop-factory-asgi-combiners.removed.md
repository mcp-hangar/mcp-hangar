**core:** `create_health_routes`, `create_combined_asgi_app`, `create_auth_combined_app` and `MCPServerFactory.create_asgi_app` are removed from `mcp_hangar.fastmcp_server`. They were the factory's own ASGI assembly, which the shipped gateway never used — `serve --http` builds its app in `server/lifecycle.mcp_app_for_serving` and wraps it with `create_auth_enforced_app`. The two had drifted: the deleted routes were flat `/health` and `/ready`, while a running Hangar serves `/health/live`, `/health/ready` and `/health/startup`. Nothing about the served app changes; see `UPGRADE.md`
