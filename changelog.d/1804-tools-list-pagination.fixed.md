An upstream that paginates `tools/list` is now read to its last page. Only the first page was read, so a tool on a later page was not listed, not pinned by `mcp-hangar pin`, not checked for drift (the periodic re-check included) and could not be called through Hangar. Pages are followed through `nextCursor`, up to 50 pages or until a cursor repeats, with a warning (`tools_list_pagination_stopped`) if the walk is cut short; a page that errors fails the listing as a failed single call did.
