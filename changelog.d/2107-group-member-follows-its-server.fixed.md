**core:** A deleted server leaves every group it was a member of. Before, a group kept the stopped copy in rotation after `DELETE /api/mcp_servers/{id}` or a discovery TTL removal, and the next call through the group started the deleted server again, outside the fleet, where nothing managed it. The member now stays in the group as a vacant member (`"vacant": true` and state `cold` in the group status) that no call, rebalance, start or recovery probe reaches, and a server registered again under that id fills it, back in the rotation it had. Other replicas do the same when they learn of the deletion or registration, and now close the dropped copy's client instead of leaking it.
