**core:** the front door's `tools/list`, `prompts/list`, `resources/list`, `resources/templates/list` and `server/discover` decide each name on the member a call would be routed to, through the `decide()` a call makes. A member of one group was listed when its own policy allowed a name, whichever member a call then selected; a name is now listed only when every member a call for this tenant may select allows it (the pinned or canary member alone for a routed tenant), and a group with no member in rotation lists nothing, as a call to it is refused. A prompt or a resource of a group was decided under the group alone, so a member's own policy never applied, and was relayed to a member chosen without the tenant; it is now decided on the members a call may select, a fetch selects its member as a tool call does, and a listing is answered by a running member without advancing the strategy or starting a member.
