**core:** In `tool_access.mode: front_door`, a boot warm-up that never finishes, for example on an upstream handshake that never returns, no longer makes every empty `tools/list` or `server/discover` wait 5 s for the life of the process. The warming state now ends at a bound derived from the servers being warmed: each server's start budget (one attempt at each of its two start calls, at the stdio client's 15 s or at a `remote` server's `http.connect_timeout` plus `http.read_timeout`, never less than 30 s), summed because they start one at a time, plus 30 s. An image pull or a retried `remote` call can outlast it; that only ends the wait early. When it passes, listings stop waiting and a `front_door_warmup_bound_passed` WARNING names the servers still warming. A server the warm-up starts after the bound is still projected and announced with `notifications/tools/list_changed`.
