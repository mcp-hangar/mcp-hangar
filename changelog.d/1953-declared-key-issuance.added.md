**core:** a key-pinned server may declare where its keys come from, `tls.peer_identity.issuance: kbs-attested` (ADR-030 §2). The value is one of a closed set, and anything else is refused at load. A call to such a server records it as `declared_key_issuance` on `ToolInvocationCompleted` and `ToolInvocationFailed`. The JSON-lines, LEEF, syslog and OTLP exports carry it; CEF has no free field for it. It is a declaration that Hangar records and does not verify. It changes neither the identity digest pins bind to nor the keys the handshake accepts. `GET /api/system/` lists the `declared_key_issuance` capability.
