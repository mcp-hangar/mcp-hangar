**core:** `McpServerFailoverSaga` and `McpServerFailoverEventSaga` are deprecated and warn when configured. No configuration key reaches `configure_failover()`, so a running gateway never fails over through them, and the step saga's `failback` stops the backup right after `start_backup` started it, because `await_primary` does not wait. Both stay importable and behave as before; `configure_failover()` and the step saga's `configure()` raise a `DeprecationWarning`. A group's rotation is the failover Hangar has: put the primary and the backup in a group.
