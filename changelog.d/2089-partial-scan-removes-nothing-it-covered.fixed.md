**core:** An authoritative discovery source no longer removes servers because part of its scan failed. A Kubernetes namespace whose pods cannot be listed, or a discovery file that exists but cannot be read or parsed, keeps what it registered -- neither removed nor refreshed -- and a WARNING names the part and the servers kept, every cycle. When `namespaces` is unset and the namespace listing itself fails, the scan now fails as a whole and removes nothing, and one pod whose annotations do not parse no longer hides the rest of its namespace. A deleted file, a disabled definition and a file refused by the trust checks still remove their server after its TTL.
