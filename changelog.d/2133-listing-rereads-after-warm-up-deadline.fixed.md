**core:** On a front door, a first `tools/list` or flat `tools/call` that waited out the boot warm-up's 5 s deadline answered with the empty catalogue it read before waiting, even when some servers had been projected during the wait. It now re-reads after any wait and serves what exists, as `server/discover` already did.
