Withdrawing or restoring a tool, prompt or resource for every tenant at once (`POST /api/admin/tools/{server}/{tool}/withdraw|restore` with no `tenant_id`) now needs the administrator's grant. It was open to any global `developer`, whose `mcp_servers:lifecycle` is there to start and stop servers, and let one take a tool away from the whole fleet. A global grant naming a tenant, and a tenant-scoped grant acting on its own tenant, are unchanged.
