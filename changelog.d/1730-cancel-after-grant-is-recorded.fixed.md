A call whose batch is cancelled after its approval was granted -- during the re-checks that follow the hold, or the cold start -- no longer leaves a grant that took effect in the audit trail. `ToolApprovalGranted` is still published (the human did approve), then the record moves from approved to cancelled and `ToolApprovalCancelled` is published, reason "approved, then the call was cancelled before it ran". A cancel during the hold is unchanged (#1702).
