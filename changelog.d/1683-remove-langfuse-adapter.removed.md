**core:** the Langfuse adapter is removed, with its configuration. Nothing called it after 2.22.0, so `observability.langfuse`, `MCP_LANGFUSE_*` and `HANGAR_LANGFUSE_*` sent nothing to Langfuse, and `Runtime.observability` was built and never read. Langfuse takes Hangar's spans over OTLP instead: point `OTEL_EXPORTER_OTLP_TRACES_ENDPOINT` at its OTLP endpoint (`examples/langfuse/README.md`). A removed scrub setting (`MCP_LANGFUSE_SCRUB_*`, `HANGAR_LANGFUSE_SCRUB_*`, `observability.langfuse.scrub_*`) now refuses the boot, whatever its value, so a deployment that asked for scrubbing finds out the setting no longer applies; the other removed settings are named in a `langfuse_settings_removed` warning, and `config check` names the `observability.langfuse` block. The `langfuse` extra is gone. This also closes the typo that turned scrubbing off (#1655) and the second configuration path (#1666) (#1683).
