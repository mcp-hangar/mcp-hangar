`GET /api/admin/tools/pins?tool=NAME[&server=ID]` returns the history of a tool's pin (recorded, re-approved, revoked) from the pin-ledger streams of the event store, oldest first, for a caller with `audit:read`; a tenant-scoped grant sees its own tenant's pins only. The CEF export now gives `ToolPinRecorded` and `ToolPinRevoked` their own signatures (410, 411) and carries the pinned digest (`fileHash`), the one it replaced (`oldFileHash`) and the approver (`flexString2`).
