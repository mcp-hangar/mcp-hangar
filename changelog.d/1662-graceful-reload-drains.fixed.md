A graceful reload (`POST /api/config/reload`, `hangar_reload_config` with `graceful`) waits for the calls in flight to the servers it is about to stop, for up to 30 seconds in all, before stopping them. Both reload modes used to stop at once, so `graceful` drained nothing. A forced reload (`graceful: false`) still stops at once, and a call that outlives the wait is logged as `reload_drain_timed_out` and cut off.
