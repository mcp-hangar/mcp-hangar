**core:** A refusal raised outside the gates now reads like a gate's (ADR-029 s12, #1918). A `tool:invoke` denial, an L7 `deny` or `require_approval`, an upstream key mismatch at dispatch and a command-bus rate limit start with `Refused by MCP Hangar (<layer>): <reason>. <next step>`, with the message they had on the next line and `_meta["io.mcp-hangar/toolFailure"]`; a `tool:invoke` denial also carries `io.mcp-hangar/authorizationDenial`. A rate limit is `class: reliability` with `retryNotBefore`. A rate limit and a key mismatch at dispatch now publish `ToolCallRefused`, so a rate-limited call writes a durable event and an audit record; `mcp-hangar why` and the audit show a key mismatch once.
