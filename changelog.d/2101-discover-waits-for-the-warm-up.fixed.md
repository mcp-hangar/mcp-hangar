**core:** In `tool_access.mode: front_door`, `GET`/`POST /server/discover` now waits out the boot warm-up the way `tools/list` does, on the same condition and for at most the same 5 seconds. Before, a discover that arrived after the gateway answered `/health/live` but before the warm-up had projected the upstreams returned an empty `tools` list, while the same caller's `tools/list` a moment later returned the full catalogue. A caller with no identity, a catalogue that policy leaves empty for the caller, and an `egress` gateway are still answered at once.
