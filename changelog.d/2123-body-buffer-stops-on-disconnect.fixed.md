**core:** a client that disconnects in the middle of sending a legacy-era (2025-06-18) `POST /mcp` with an `Mcp-Method` or `Mcp-Name` header no longer stops the HTTP gateway. The SEP-2243 routing wrap buffered the body in a loop that skipped `http.disconnect`, and uvicorn answers every later `receive()` with a disconnect without yielding, so the loop held the event loop for good and the gateway answered nothing else. The buffering now ends at the disconnect and the request is dropped without an answer.
