Projected lists (`tools/list`, `prompts/list`, `resources/list`, `resources/templates/list`) now carry the SEP-2549 `cacheScope` and `ttlMs` at the top level of the result, where a client or cache reads them, instead of in `_meta`. A client used to see `ttlMs: 0` and refetch on every call; it now sees `cacheScope: "private"` and `ttlMs: 5000`. The per-tenant `cacheScope` token that was in `_meta` is gone: `"private"` already means "not shared across authorization contexts".
