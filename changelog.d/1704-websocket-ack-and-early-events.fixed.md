`/api/ws/events`: the `subscribed` ack now lists in `event_types` only the patterns that are active and names the rest in a new `rejected_event_types` key (a wildcard must be a whole segment, so `ToolApproval*` matches nothing and was echoed as if subscribed). Events published between connect and the client's subscribe message are no longer lost; they are queued and filtered by the subscription when sent.
