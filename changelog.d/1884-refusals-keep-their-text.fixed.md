A caller refused by `hangar_*` authorization, by a suspended session or by an invalid argument is told why again. With mcp 2.3.0 the SDK reports any exception a tool raises, other than its own `ToolError`, as `Error executing tool <name>` and nothing more, so a denied client saw no reason. These refusals are now raised as the SDK's `ToolError`, which carries their text (still a refusal; nothing is let through). `examples/provider_math` reports division by zero the same way.
