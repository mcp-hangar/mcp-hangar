**core:** a stdio `serve` exits on SIGTERM or SIGINT while its client keeps stdin open. It shut its servers down and then kept running until stdin closed or it got a SIGKILL, because the interpreter waited for the SDK's stdin reader, a thread blocked in a read. The process now exits with 0 at most 2 seconds after the shutdown, logging `stdio_exit_forced` when it had to end the blocked reader; closing stdin still exits as before, and HTTP mode is unchanged.
