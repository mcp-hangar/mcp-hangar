Hangar's governance extensions now carry SEP-2133 identifiers, `io.mcp-hangar/validator`, `io.mcp-hangar/mutator` and `io.mcp-hangar/digest-pinning`, instead of the dotted `io.mcp-hangar.<name>`, which has no `{vendor-prefix}/{extension-name}` form. `server/discover` (the 2026-07-28 surface) advertises them under `capabilities.extensions`; `initialize` keeps them under `capabilities.experimental`, together with the dotted forms marked `deprecated` for one minor. `interceptors/list` lists the new identifiers and `interceptors/invoke` still accepts the dotted ones.
