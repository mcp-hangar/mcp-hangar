**core:** Over stdio Hangar now ends an approval's wait itself, after `approvals.stdio_wait_seconds` (default 50), before the 60 s the MCP TypeScript SDK gives a request (ADR-029 s12.4, #1918). The caller gets the `approval` refusal with reason `approval_timeout` instead of a transport timeout. Where the limit is shorter than `approval_timeout_seconds` it ends the approval's window: the record moves to `expired`, `ToolApprovalExpired` is published, and a later `mcp-hangar approvals approve` is refused.
