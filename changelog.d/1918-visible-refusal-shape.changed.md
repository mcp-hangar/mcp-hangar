**core:** a gate that refuses a tool the caller may see now says so in a bounded first line, `Refused by MCP Hangar (<gate>): <reason>. <next step>`, above the gate's own message, and carries `io.mcp-hangar/toolFailure` in `_meta`, with `io.mcp-hangar/authorizationDenial` on a denied approval (ADR-029 section 12). On `hangar_call` each refused entry of `results` carries them. Refusals outside the gates follow separately.
