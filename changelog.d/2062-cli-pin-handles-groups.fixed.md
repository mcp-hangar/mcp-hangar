**cli:** `mcp-hangar pin` handles a `mode: group` entry. It used to build the group as a server, so any configuration with a group made `pin`, `pin --write` and `pin --check` exit 2 (`unsupported_mode: group`), real drift on another server was reported as 2 instead of 1, and the pins declared on the group itself were never compared. A group is no longer started: its members are asked, a member defined inline in the group included, and `pin --check` holds the group's own `tool_projection.pins` (and `upstream`) against every member that serves the pinned tool, as the gateway does. A drift on a group names the member in the text report and as `member` in `--json`. `--write` writes no pins into a group or into an inline member, and the members a group's `auto_start` started while the file was loaded are stopped again
