**cli:** `mcp-hangar approvals list|approve|deny` resolves a call held by a gateway that runs over stdio, which serves no REST API, so until now a call on an `approval_list` could only time out. The command opens the approval store the gateway shares (the selected `persistence.backend`, or `mcp_hangar.db` beside an absolute `event_store.path`; `--db` names a file) and decides through the same resolution chokepoint as `POST /api/approvals/{id}/resolve`, so expiry, the compare-and-swap and tenant scoping are unchanged. The waiting gateway sees the decision on its next poll of the store, about two seconds, and publishes the usual `ToolApprovalGranted` or `ToolApprovalDenied`. No listener or IPC channel is added. The approver is always the declared `auth.stdio.principal`, and a configuration without one is refused. The principal's declared roles must hold `approval:resolve` (`provider-admin` or `admin`), and that holds even with auth off: `viewer`, the role `init` declares, is refused with the role it needs and the key to change.
